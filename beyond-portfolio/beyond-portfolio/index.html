About

I'm Shanmukha Krishna — the person behind BEYOND.

I've spent the last three years inside AI tools: not just using them, but tracking how fast they change and what they're actually good for. At 17, I run two YouTube channels where I write the script, generate the voice, and produce the visuals myself — without ever stepping in front of a camera.

I can read and write basic code — C, Python, Java, HTML and CSS — but most of what I build happens through AI. I'm a builder who works through AI first: turning an idea into a finished video, a working tool, or a piece of content using whatever the fastest, sharpest tool for the job is.

I don't just want to watch this technology grow. I want to build with it, every day.
