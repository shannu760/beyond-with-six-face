### Ⅰ. CODE (Software Architecture)
- **Engine**: Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, React 18.
- **Visual Computing**: Three.js WebGL 3D Fragment Portal & Canvas 2D Orbital Arcs particle physics.
- **Motion Science**: `motion/animateView` (Native View Transitions API) & `inView` high-performance scroll triggers.
- **Optimization**: Zero-lag GPU rendering via IntersectionObserver render-pausing & capped DPR (`1.5`).

### Ⅱ. DESIGN (Visual & Spatial Aesthetic)
- **Palette**: Editorial Olive & Gold design system (`#252B18` Dark Olive, `#3D4425` Deep Olive, `#C8A95B` Gold, `#E8DCC3` Beige).
- **Surface Geometry**: Subtle film grain overlays, backdrop glassmorphism, responsive grid boundaries.
- **Typography**: Inter, Cormorant Garamond, and Geist Mono SemiBold.

### Ⅲ. AI (Generative Engineering)
- **Agent Integration**: Built for multi-agent workflows, prompt engineering, and intelligent asset generation.
- **Future Integration**: Real-time voice synthesis and interactive "Chat with Portfolio Data".

### Ⅳ. CONTENT & MEDIA (Storytelling Ecosystem)
- **GOD EDITZ**: High-octane cinematic video editing, CapCut & CapCut Pro mastery, Open Montage sequences, and Anti-Gravity transitions.
- **THE_DIMENSIONLESS**: Digital essays exploring the intersection of AI, technology, and human potential.

## 📐 3. Feature Architecture & System Design

### 1. Interactive 3D WebGL Portal (`PortalCanvas.tsx`)
- Multi-ring rotators, floating geometric fragments, and dynamic mouse tracking.
- Lerped scroll scaling (`1.0` ➔ `0.6`) synchronized with hero scroll progress.
- Auto-pausing render loop when off-screen to preserve 100% CPU/GPU performance.

### 2. Canvas 2D Orbital Arcs (`OrbitalArcs.tsx`)
- Dual-color data pixel particles orbiting randomized elliptical trajectories with mouse interaction ripples.
- Real-time resolution scaling based on device pixel ratio.

### 3. Direct Client Gmail Integration (`ContactSection.tsx`)
- One-click direct client mailing to `krishna.addanki.633@gmail.com`.
- Pre-filled custom query subject & body generation for direct client communication.

### 4. Cinematic 404 Video Experience (`not-found.tsx`)
- Single-page full-viewport background video (`https://d8j0ntlcm91z4.cloudfront.net/...`).
- Custom `"Geist Mono:SemiBold"` typography with clipping gradient text (`295px` font scale).
- Official BEYOND circular seal emblem logo header.
